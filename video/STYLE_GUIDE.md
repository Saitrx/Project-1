# Style guide (from the reference video)

Reference: 61 s, 720x1280, 30 fps. Frames studied at 2 fps plus full-res close-ups.

## Character design and proportions
- Faceless block people, Roblox-like. The head is a softly rounded cube with a separate hair "cap"
  block (black or brown). No facial features.
- One flat, saturated colour per garment: shirts in sky blue, orange, yellow, teal; trousers in
  navy or maroon; dark shoe blocks. Short sleeves leave skin-tone block forearms and hands.
- About 6.5-7 heads tall. Legs are roughly half the height; shoulders about 1.6 head-widths.
  Kids are about two thirds the height of adults, with bigger heads.
- First-person shots show a chunky mannequin-style hand with capsule fingers and a dark sleeve.

## How people walk, run and react
- Limbs swing from the shoulder and hip like a puppet. Segments stay mostly stiff, arms swing
  opposite the legs, steps are short.
- Runners are seen from behind, leaning forward with pumping arms.
- Reactions are big, readable whole-body poses: both arms straight up, a stiff fall backwards,
  people standing still and staring. There are long holds on people simply standing, with a
  slight sway.

## Camera
- Mostly first-person eye height (about 1.6 m), handheld, with gentle drift and sway.
- Slow pans and push-ins, following characters from behind, low wide shots.
- Very few hard cuts (2 in 61 s). Beats flow through camera moves, whip pans with motion blur
  and soft dissolves. Light shake only on the big moment.

## Lighting, colours and materials
- Soft daylight, hazy atmosphere, long soft-edged shadows.
- Matte, flat-coloured materials with almost no texture. Bright clothes stand out against muted
  beige ground and a pale sea.
- Washed-out, low-contrast "milky" grade with lifted blacks. Shallow depth of field: the
  foreground and background are often soft.
- The ending moves to dark teal, then a charcoal (#0f0f0f) title card.

## Pacing and cuts
- About 12 beats in 61 s. Each caption holds 3-5 s, one idea per beat, constant gentle motion,
  ending on a title card.

## Caption and text style
- Captions: white serif italic (Garamond-like), about 4% of frame width, one short line,
  centred at about 67% of frame height, soft dark shadow behind, about 0.3 s fade in and out.
- HUD at top-left (about 16% down): tiny letter-spaced sans caps label, a big upright serif value,
  a tiny letter-spaced caps subline, and a soft dark radial patch behind it for legibility.
- Title: large upright serif, two lines, centred near the top at the start. The end card uses the
  same serif centred on charcoal with a tiny tracked-caps line below.
- Sound: ambient sound and music, no voiceover.

## How this video applies it
- Same block-character kit: men, women in loose long-sleeved tops and long flared skirts with
  visible hair, kids, workers in hi-vis vests and hard hats, a suited leader.
- Knees: the reference legs are mostly stiff pivots. The brief asks for bending knees, so segments
  keep the blocky look but knees flex moderately in swing. This doesn't change the look and reads
  as a natural walk. The run uses a forward lean, high knees and arms bent about 80 degrees.
- Camera: eye-height handheld drift (seeded noise), slow push-ins, short dissolves between beats
  instead of hard cuts, light shake only when the doors lock.
- Grade: ACES filmic, slightly desaturated, lifted milky blacks, gentle vignette, haze/fog on
  every scene. Night scenes keep the same soft, hazy look, lit only by windows, lamps and screens.
- HUD: the reference layout at top-left. Label "SINCE THE SWITCH", big serif "DAY 1" ... "YEAR 100",
  and a small CO2 subline. It changes only on cuts.
- Captions: serif italic, white, soft shadow, about 67% height, short lines, fades.
- Title card at the start in the reference title style. The last line uses the end-card style.
- Sound: procedural ambient pad plus scene sound effects, no voiceover, and true digital silence for
  the "Us." beat.
- Nothing copied: no logos, brand names, watermarks or channel names.
