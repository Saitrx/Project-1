# Posting checklist (TikTok)

**Pick the files**
- [ ] With narrator: `output/parts/school_voiceover_part1.mp4`, `_part2.mp4`, `_part3.mp4`.
- [ ] Sound effects only: `output/parts/school_sfx_part1.mp4`, `_part2.mp4`, `_part3.mp4`. Use them with
  on-screen captions only, or with your own voice recorded in the TikTok app.
- The full video is split in 3 because GitHub refuses files over 100 MB. Nothing is lost: the video
  is copied, not re-encoded, and the 3 parts put together are the full video, frame for frame.
  - **One TikTok:** on the upload screen select all 3 parts in order (part 1, 2, 3); TikTok joins
    them. CapCut does the same if you want to join them first.
  - **A 3-part series:** the cuts fall between narration lines (0:34 after "No alarms", 0:58 after
    "we slowly slide back"), so each part also stands on its own.
- All parts are FHD 1080x1920, 60 fps, H.264 at 20 Mbps with AAC 320 kbps: 34 s (90 MB),
  24 s (48 MB) and 27.5 s (79 MB).
- The TikTok phone app may refuse big files on some phones (older Android limits were about 72 MB).
  If it does, upload from a computer at tiktok.com/upload, which takes much larger files.

**Before uploading**
- [ ] Watch the file once on your phone, start to finish, with sound on.
  - The captions sit in the lower-middle area. They stay clear of the bottom 20% and the right 15%,
    so TikTok's buttons and description don't cover them.
- [ ] Upload from the phone's gallery, or from desktop at tiktok.com/upload.
  - In the app, turn on **Settings → Data saver: off** and **Upload HD** (or "Allow high-quality
    uploads"), so TikTok keeps the 1080p quality.
- [ ] Don't add TikTok's own music. The video has no music on purpose; the sounds are part of the
  story.
- [ ] If you add TikTok's auto-captions, turn them off again. The captions are already burned in,
  and two sets would overlap.

**Post details**
- [ ] Cover: `output/school_cover.png` (1080x1920, the title over the school crumbling at 0:08).
  In the upload screen tap **Select cover / Edit cover**, then **Upload** (or "Add from gallery")
  and pick the PNG. The title sits inside the 3:4 profile-grid crop, so it stays readable in your
  grid. If your app only lets you pick a frame, take the one at about 0:08.
- [ ] Caption idea: "What if every school disappeared overnight? 📚💨 #whatif #school #education
  #kids #animation #storytime"
- [ ] Hook: the first line is spoken in the first second ("What if all the schools disappeared?").
  Don't trim the start.
- [ ] Facts on screen and their sources:
  - 2020 school closures affected over 1.5 billion students (UNESCO, 2020).
  - About 12% of people could read in 1820, versus about 87% today (Our World in Data, literacy
    rate).
  - Put "Sources: UNESCO, Our World in Data" in a comment if anyone asks.
- [ ] Audience: general. No people are hurt (the kids land softly on the grass), and there is no
  music to clear.

**After posting**
- [ ] Check the posted video on another phone for sound sync and caption position.
- [ ] Pin a comment with the question from the end: "Would you still wish for it?"
