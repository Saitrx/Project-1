# Style notes: reference "What if the earthquakes kept getting stronger?"

Reference: 84 s, 576x1024, 30 fps. Frames studied at 2 fps (168 frames, 11 contact sheets) plus
full-res close-ups of the HUD, captions, hands, fall, ending and end card.

## First-person camera, arms and hands
- One continuous first-person POV at eye height (~1.6 m), walking down a New York sidewalk.
- Natural head bob and side-to-side sway while walking. The head often tilts up to look at
  buildings and the sky, and glances left at passing cars. Fast head turns get motion blur.
- Our own shadow (head, shoulders, swinging arms) falls ahead of us on the sidewalk in hard sunlight.
- Arms and hands are blocky low-poly: a dark navy sleeve box, a peach palm box, four separate
  block fingers and a block thumb. Hands come up palms-out to shield the face; while running, the
  arms pump into the bottom corners; when we fall, both hands slam flat onto the asphalt and the
  camera looks down at them, then looks up again.

## City look, lighting and colour
- Bright, sunny, saturated, flat-shaded low-poly Manhattan: red brick walk-ups with black fire
  escapes, cream/beige stone blocks with punched windows, cyan glass towers, green awnings,
  red hanging shop signs, green subway entrance globes, yellow taxis, beige/white/red sedans,
  white box trucks, green blobby trees, a hot-dog cart with a striped umbrella, a red hydrant.
- Chrysler-like silver spire at the end of the avenue, slightly left of centre.
- Hard, crisp sun shadows (diagonal pole and building shadows across the light grey sidewalk),
  deep blue sky, light haze toward the horizon.
- As the destruction grows the air fills with warm brown/tan dust: sun goes orange, everything
  desaturates toward sepia, big soft dust clouds linger, debris (brick-red and beige chunks)
  tumbles. Glowing orange cracks appear at the very end of the city sequence.

## HUD and captions
- HUD on the LEFT at mid-height (label at ~47% height, x ~6%): tiny uppercase letter-spaced sans
  label ("MAGNITUDE"), a large serif number below ("5.7") that counts up smoothly, then a tiny
  uppercase letter-spaced fact line ("ENERGY x12,000"). Faint dark shadow behind it.
- Captions: white italic serif (Garamond-like), one short line, centred, ~67% height in the
  reference (we use ~62% as requested), soft fade in/out, soft shadow.
- Title: white upright serif, two lines, top-centre (~23% height), over the first walking shot.

## Pacing and cuts
- About one beat every 5-7 s; each beat = a caption + a new counter value.
- Mostly continuous walking takes, with hard cuts between some beats (cut to the roadway, cut
  to looking up at buildings). Camera shake grows with the magnitude; impacts get short jolts.

## Screen-crack ending and end card
- The world dissolves into a black star field; white glass-crack lines spiral/radiate outward
  from the centre like a spider web, then the HUD reads "MAGNITUDE 1,000 / HERE" over a
  dark grey cracked screen with two or three spider-web impact points (bright white centres),
  a few black glass shards falling away.
- End card: near-black (#111) background, the title in upright serif centred around 40%
  height, and two lines of tiny grey monospace caps sources underneath.

## What we do NOT copy
- No TikTok logo, no "@man_ia_c" watermark, no "AI-generated" label, no brand names or logos.

## Decisions for "What if cars kept getting bigger?"
- Same POV language: blocky navy-sleeve arms, peach block hands, own shadow ahead, head bob.
- Same city palette and hard sun; warm dust from the 10x beat onward.
- HUD: "CAR SIZE" / serif "1.2x" / fact line, left at mid-height; captions italic serif at 62%.
- All text drawn on a separate 4K canvas composited after every image effect; nothing in the
  bottom 20% or right 15% of the frame (TikTok UI).
- Ending: black, two headlights come on once (slow fade, no strobe), the screen cracks into
  spider-web impacts like the reference, HUD "CAR SIZE: TOO BIG / HERE", then the end card.
